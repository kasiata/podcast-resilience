    Your resignation letter

    Most people leave art quietly: fewer projects, an application not sent, a studio given up. Nobody writes a letter, so nobody has to say what they are giving back. This one says it. It lists the five things you packed at the start, because those are what you lose when you stop. Read it, change any line, print it if you like. Then decide whether you sign it or withdraw it.
